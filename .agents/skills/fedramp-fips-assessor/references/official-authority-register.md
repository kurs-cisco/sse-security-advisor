# Official authority register

All entries were retrieved **2026-09-18**. Preserve these URLs and retrieval
date in the assessment run. Re-check an authority if the assessment is rerun
after that date or if a policy decision depends on changed guidance.

| Authority | URL | Assessment use |
|---|---|---|
| NIST CMVP program | https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/cryptographic-module-validation-program | States the 2026-09-21 last date for FIPS 140-2 active modules in new systems and the 2026-09-22 move to the Historical List. |
| NIST CMVP FAQ | https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/cryptographic-module-validation-program/faqs | Confirms that only FIPS 140-3 validations remain on the active list from 2026-09-22. |
| NIST FIPS 140-3 transition effort | https://csrc.nist.gov/Projects/FIPS-140-3-Transition-Effort | Describes the transition timeline and continued use context for existing systems. |
| NIST FIPS 140-2 management manual | https://csrc.nist.gov/csrc/media/Projects/cryptographic-module-validation-program/documents/FIPS-140-2%20CMVP%20Management%20Manual%2003-10-2023.pdf | States that historical-list modules should not be considered for new systems. |
| NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 | https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final | Primary NIST control catalog source; SC-13 remains a candidate mapping and the organization’s adopted baseline/control implementation must be validated separately. |

The public transition material does not resolve whether a particular service is
within the ATO boundary or whether a particular POA&M is required. Those remain
organization and authorized-assessor/AO decisions.
